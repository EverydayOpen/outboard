What it moves, guides and refuses

Outboard knows 15 apps. The catalogue is plain data in the source, one entry per folder, checked by tests and by a validator on every change. Anyone can propose an entry with a pull request; each one needs sources.

Not yet tried on a real Mac. An entry is marked as tried only after a named tester has run it and the public verify log says so. The moves are rolled out in order: Xcode build data, then the caches that use a link, then iPhone backups, then Xcode archives.

Moves Outboard does itself (7)

Copied to your drive, compared file by file, switched over, and kept as an original until you confirm. Each one is hidden in the app until you turn on "Show moves not yet tried on a real Mac", because none has been run on a real Mac yet. "Official setting" means the app has a setting for it that Outboard points at the copy; "Community method" means a link, which is not a setting the app documents.

  • Xcode build data~/Library/Developer/Xcode/DerivedDataOfficial settingRebuilds itself

    If the drive is away: While Outboard is running, Xcode goes back to its normal build folder on your Mac until the drive returns.

    Confidence in the method: medium. Rollout step 1 of 4. Not yet tried on a real Mac.

  • Hugging Face models~/.cache/huggingface/hubCommunity methodLarge to download again

    If the drive is away: Tools that use the Hugging Face cache stop with an error, or download again to your Mac, until the drive returns.

    Confidence in the method: high. Rollout step 2 of 4. Not yet tried on a real Mac.

  • Ollama models~/.ollama/modelsCommunity methodLarge to download again

    If the drive is away: Ollama won't find its models, and new downloads may land on your Mac, until the drive returns.

    Confidence in the method: high. Rollout step 2 of 4. Not yet tried on a real Mac.

  • llama.cpp models~/Library/Caches/llama.cppCommunity methodLarge to download again

    If the drive is away: llama.cpp can't find the models it downloaded before, and may download them again to your Mac.

    Confidence in the method: high. Rollout step 2 of 4. Not yet tried on a real Mac.

  • npm cache~/.npmCommunity methodRebuilds itself

    If the drive is away: npm can't use its cache and fetches packages again, or reports an error, until the drive returns.

    Confidence in the method: high. Rollout step 2 of 4. Not yet tried on a real Mac.

  • iPhone backups~/Library/Application Support/MobileSync/BackupCommunity methodCan't be replaced

    If the drive is away: Finder and Apple Devices can't find your backups, and may not be able to back up a device, until the drive returns.

    Confidence in the method: medium. Rollout step 3 of 4. Not yet tried on a real Mac.

  • Xcode archives~/Library/Developer/Xcode/ArchivesOfficial settingCan't be replaced

    If the drive is away: Xcode's Organizer can't list these archives until the drive returns.

    Confidence in the method: medium. Rollout step 4 of 4. Not yet tried on a real Mac.

Guided, by the app itself (8)

These apps have their own setting for a second location. Outboard moves nothing: it shows the steps, with a button that opens the app, and measures the folder so you know what to expect.

  • App Store apps over 1 GBGuidedRebuilds itself

    3 steps. The app does the moving; Outboard shows the steps and opens it.

    If the drive is away: macOS prompts you to download the app again.

  • Photos library~/Pictures/Photos Library.photoslibraryGuidedCan't be replaced

    4 steps. The app does the moving; Outboard shows the steps and opens it.

    If the drive is away: Photos stops using the library and starts a new empty one in the default place.

  • Music media folder~/Music/Music/MediaGuidedLarge to download again

    4 steps. The app does the moving; Outboard shows the steps and opens it.

    If the drive is away: Tracks stored on the drive may show as missing until it returns.

  • Final Cut Pro librariesGuidedCan't be replaced

    4 steps. The app does the moving; Outboard shows the steps and opens it.

    If the drive is away: The library is missing from the sidebar until the drive returns.

  • Logic Pro Sound Library~/Music/Logic Pro Library.bundleGuidedLarge to download again

    4 steps. The app does the moving; Outboard shows the steps and opens it.

    If the drive is away: Logic Pro asks you to Retry, Ignore (basic tones) or Reset (reinstall the library on your Mac).

  • Steam games~/Library/Application Support/Steam/steamappsGuidedLarge to download again

    4 steps. The app does the moving; Outboard shows the steps and opens it.

    If the drive is away: Games show as not installed until Steam sees the folder again.

  • LM Studio models~/.lmstudio/modelsGuidedLarge to download again

    4 steps. The app does the moving; Outboard shows the steps and opens it.

    If the drive is away: The models on the drive don't appear in LM Studio until it returns.

  • Android SDK~/Library/Android/sdkGuidedLarge to download again

    5 steps. The app does the moving; Outboard shows the steps and opens it.

    If the drive is away: Android Studio and Gradle can't find the SDK until the drive returns.

Not offered (9)

A visible card with the reason, so you are not left wondering why a big folder is missing from the plan.

  • Sandboxed app dataNot offered

    macOS stops sandboxed apps from starting when their data folder is replaced by a link, as Docker for Mac issue 771 reports.

  • Mail, Safari, Messages and NotesNot offered

    Apple apps expect these on your Mac, and macOS protects them. Outboard doesn't touch them.

  • HomebrewNot offered

    Homebrew's prebuilt packages only work in its default location on your Mac's own disk (Homebrew docs).

  • The Caches folder and your home folderNot offered

    Hundreds of apps write to the Caches folder the moment they start, so moving all of it makes apps misbehave when the drive is away. Individual app caches are offered where Outboard knows how. Your home folder isn't offered either: Apple supports moving it, and with FileVault it can stop you logging in.

  • Anything in iCloud DriveNot offered

    These files are managed by iCloud. Moving them can interfere with syncing.

  • Apps themselvesNot offered

    Moving an app can break its updates and permissions. Outboard moves data, not apps.

  • Xcode simulator runtimesNot offered

    Xcode manages simulator runtimes as disk images. Moving them can leave simulators unable to load. To free space, remove the runtimes you don't need in Xcode, Settings, Components.

  • Docker Desktop and OrbStackNot offered

    Docker Desktop and OrbStack have their own storage setting, and Docker's has been unreliable on external drives for years. Outboard doesn't move their data.

  • pnpm and uv storesNot offered

    These stores work by linking files from your projects. On another drive the files are copied instead, so space on your Mac can go up, not down.
